Privacy Policy – LineGods

Last updated: November 10, 2026

LineGods (“the game”) is made by Oblivion Software LLC, an Idaho company that publishes games as Oblivion Soft (“we”, “us”). It is run by independent developer Adam Allen.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

  • Your settings and progress (volume, difficulty, commander level, achievements, campaign stars) are stored on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may copy them to your own Google account so they come back on a new phone; we never see them.
  • The optional Full Game unlock is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the game only asks Google Play whether this account owns the unlock. We never see your payment details. Google’s handling of the purchase is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • The game works fully offline and has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking.

Permissions

The Android app asks for internet access only so that Google Play Billing can check and complete purchases.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if online features or Google Play Games are added), we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
